About & Research

I am a first-year CS PhD student at University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), advised by Prof. Nanyun (Violet) Peng and Prof. Wei Wang. I work in PLUS Lab and ScAI Lab, alongside many supportive and talented people.

My research interests broadly lie in Agentic Systems, Agent Memory, and Retrieval-Augmented LLMs/LVLMs. My recent work explores these topics in the context of AI for Open-ended Research.

  • Agentic systems
  • Agent memory
